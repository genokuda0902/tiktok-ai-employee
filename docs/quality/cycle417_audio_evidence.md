# Cycle 417 — audio QA

Technical audio presence does not establish Japanese speech or subtitle alignment.

Required independent checks: scene-by-scene audio energy, clipping, duration, and source provenance. Audio with only sound effects must remain `JAPANESE_NARRATION=ABSENT`, `SEMANTIC_SYNC=UNVERIFIED`, and `PUBLICATION_NOT_APPROVED`.

This is a review-only checkpoint, not production approval.
